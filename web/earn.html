Rewards are sent to this Solana wallet. Each wallet can only be linked to one TikTok account.

2Upload your TikTok data

Upload the .zip or .json file TikTok gives you. It's read on your device. The file itself is never uploaded anywhere.

How do I get my TikTok data?
  1. In TikTok, go to Profile → ☰ menu → Settings and privacy.
  2. Tap Account → Download your data.
  3. Choose All data and the JSON format, then tap Request data.
  4. When it's ready (a few hours to a couple of days), download it from the Download data tab.
